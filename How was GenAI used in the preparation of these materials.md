# How was GenAI used in the preparation of these materials

This system was prepared with the help of Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, primarily in writing the Second Reader Community plug-in and for advice on how to package this for distribution. Once that software was created, though, everything the user of this vault uses runs locally on their own computer. This does not require the use of involve GenAI, data centers or even an internet connection.

## Where GenAI helped

- **Second Reader**, the Obsidian plugin that links your literature notes to the readings in Zotero, brings your Zotero highlights into your notes, turns permanent note candidates into permanent notes, and packages a week's notes for upload. Claude helped write and test its code. When you use it, it runs inside Obsidian on your own computer and talks only to Zotero on your own computer.
- **This sample course.** Claude helped searched for readings on how people interact with large language models whose licenses allow them to be shared publicly, checked those licenses, and drafted the syllabus, the weekly goals and the questions to think about before class. The readings and licenses are listed in `SOURCES.md`; check anything you want to cite against the papers themselves. The instructions on how to set up the vault, etc., were written by Claude and edited by me.
- **The scripts that built this package.** These set up the vault, wrote the Zotero collection file, and downloaded the readings.

## Where it didn't

- The readings themselves. They are the published papers and preprints, unchanged.
- Your literature notes start empty, and the point of this system is to help you focus on the hard be meaningful work of getting your own understanding from what you read.
