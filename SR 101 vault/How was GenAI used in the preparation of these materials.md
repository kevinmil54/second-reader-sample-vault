# How was GenAI used in the preparation of these materials

This system was prepared with the help of Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. Most of that help went into writing programs. Those programs run locally on your computer and don't involve GenAI, data centers or an internet connection when they do their work.

## Where GenAI helped

- **Second Reader**, the Obsidian plugin that links your literature notes to the readings in Zotero, brings your Zotero highlights into your notes, turns permanent note candidates into permanent notes, and packages a week's notes for upload. Claude helped write and test its code. When you use it, it runs inside Obsidian on your own computer and talks only to Zotero on your own computer.
- **This sample course.** Claude searched for openly licensed readings on how people interact with large language models, checked their licenses, and drafted the syllabus, the weekly goals and the questions to think about before class. The readings and licenses are listed in `SOURCES.md`; check anything you want to cite against the papers themselves.
- **The scripts that built this package.** These set up the vault, wrote the Zotero import file, and downloaded the readings.

## Where it didn't

- The readings themselves. They are the published papers and preprints, unchanged.
- Your literature notes start empty. What goes in them is up to you.
