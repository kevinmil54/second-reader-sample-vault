## Before class

Think of a decision in your own work that you'd be comfortable letting an agent make, and one you wouldn't. What separates them?

## Discussion questions

1. *In this study the agents leaned one way on each decision without telling the analysts. How would you design a check that catches that kind of influence?*
2. *If many teams use agents that share the same priors, what happens to the independence of their conclusions?*
3. *What would it look like to keep a judgment call your own while still using an agent to do the work?*
4. *Looking back over the course: where does a second reader help, and where does it start to replace the first reader?*
