## Before class

Before reading, write down the three things you used an AI assistant for most recently. Keep the list; we'll compare it with what Clio found.

## Discussion questions

1. *How does what people actually ask an AI assistant compare with what you assumed they ask?*
2. *Clio uses a model to read and summarize millions of conversations so that no person has to. What does that method let the authors see, and what might it miss?*
3. *If people trust an answer more than its accuracy warrants, whose job is it to close that gap: the user, the interface, or the model?*
4. *How would you know whether your own confidence in an AI answer is well calibrated?*
