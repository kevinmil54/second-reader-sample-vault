## Before class

Think of a recent task where you used AI. What did you check, and what did you take on trust?

## Discussion questions

1. *Lee and colleagues separate confidence in the AI from confidence in yourself. Why might those pull in opposite directions?*
2. *If critical thinking moves from producing work to checking and integrating it, is that a loss, a gain, or a different skill?*
3. *Both papers rely on self-report. What would you want to measure directly, and how?*
4. *What makes offloading a task to a tool harmful in one case and sensible in another?*
