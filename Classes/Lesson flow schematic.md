### Lesson Planning Workflow: Overview & Phase 5 Artifacts

- **Phase 1: Reading Analysis and Session Framing** (Inputs: Syllabus, Lesson Sketch, Readings)
    
    - **Goal**: Conduct a deep analysis of assigned readings (_kyōzai kenkyū_) to understand the author's argument, predict student reactions, and identify the driving question (_hatsumon_) for the session.
        
- **Phase 2: Lesson Plan (Preliminary/Revised)** (Inputs: Prior Class Info, Student Reactions)
    
    - **Goal**: Create a pedagogical roadmap that includes timed segments, specific predictions of student thinking, and planned instructor moves to orchestrate discussion and synthesis.
        
- **Phase 3: Student Agenda** (Inputs: Python Agenda Script)
    
    - **Goal**: Produce a professional, three-column document (Time | Title | Details) that serves as a guide for students during the class session.
        
- **Phase 4: Slide Deck** (Inputs: Quarto File)
    
    - **Goal**: Develop a sparse, Quarto-based presentation that uses pull quotes and discussion prompts to anchor thinking without replacing verbal dialogue.
        
- **Phase 5: Post-Class Analysis** (Inputs: Lesson Transcript)
    
    - **Goal**: Analyze class transcripts and student reflections to compare predicted vs. actual student thinking, evaluate the instructor's research question, and refine the lesson for future use.
        
    - **Documents Produced**:
        
        - **Document A: Instructor Analysis** — A detailed reflection on pacing, a table comparing predicted vs. actual student thinking, and an evaluation of the session's research question.
            
        - **Document B: Student Summary** — A 2–3 page narrative summary of the session's big questions, key concepts, and connections to readings, intended as a study aid for students.
            
        - **Document C: Future Lesson Plan** — A revised version of the lesson plan that incorporates successful unplanned moves and adjustments based on what actually occurred during the session.
