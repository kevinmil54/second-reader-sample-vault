# Zana Buçinca

## About
%% author-info start %%
%% Photo: save a photo in the Authors folder and embed it on the next line, e.g. ![[Zana Buçinca.jpg|160]] %%
- **Position:** Assistant Professor, Work and Organization Studies, MIT Sloan School of Management, with a joint appointment in MIT EECS (from fall 2026)
- Buçinca studies human-AI interaction and the future of work, designing explanations and systems for AI-assisted decision-making that aim to improve both task performance and people's skills and agency. She received her PhD in computer science from Harvard in 2025, advised by Krzysztof Gajos, and was a postdoctoral researcher at Microsoft before joining MIT.
