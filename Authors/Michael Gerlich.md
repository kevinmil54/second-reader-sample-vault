# Michael Gerlich

## About
%% author-info start %%
%% Photo: save a photo in the Authors folder and embed it on the next line, e.g. ![[Michael Gerlich.jpg|160]] %%
- **Position:** Head of the Center for Strategic Corporate Foresight and Sustainability, SBS Swiss Business School (November 2025)
- Gerlich is a management researcher whose recent work deals with AI's effects on society, organizations and individuals, including public perception and acceptance of AI. He holds a PhD from Anglia Ruskin University and an MBA from the University of Cambridge, and worked for more than 30 years in business, including as a CEO, before his academic career.
